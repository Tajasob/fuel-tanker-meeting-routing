# Data licence

The archives in `data/` hold the drivable road network of Tehran extracted from OpenStreetMap, and the instances, result records and solver outputs built on it.

Road data © OpenStreetMap contributors. The data in `data/` are available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/. For OpenStreetMap copyright and attribution, see https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.

Requests, tanker fleets, costs and service durations are generated experimental inputs; the generator settings of each instance are in its `metadata.json`.
